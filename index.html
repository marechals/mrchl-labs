Operating thesis

Build where context still matters.

AI reduces the cost of building, but not the value of judgement. MRCHL Labs focuses on workflows where information is scattered, work is invisible, and decisions depend on context. The goal is to build small, useful AI-native products and services that can be tested quickly, delivered manually when needed, and automated only after value is proven.
